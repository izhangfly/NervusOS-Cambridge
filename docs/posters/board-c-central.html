How to make moneythe business model

We don't sell hardware. The body is handed over to the manufacturer, the application is handed over to the developers, and we only do the middle layer of the operating system—the money comes from application revenue sharing and factory pre-installation authorization. Traditional robots are one-time sales, and the relationship ends after the sale; with NervusOS installed, it can continuously grow new features, the money keeps flowing. We help manufacturers who only do B-end reach consumers, turning one-time hardware business into a continuously growing software ecosystem.
