Why nowwhy now

Domestic companies making robots have exceeded hundreds, with humanoid robots already crowded on the track.2025 year, Zhiyuan shipped over 5,000 units, Yushu over 4,000 units, the body is rapidly scaling up. Hardware is getting more competitive and cheaper, the truly scarce thing is that layer of general software that lets robots install apps, work — now no one has made it yet.

Robot manufacturers are stuck in B-end one-time sales, once sold, contact is cut off. We connect app stores to help them reach consumers: once a robot is sold, they can continue earning through app revenue sharing, subscriptions, and pre-installed authorization continuously earning money.

Whoever takes the ecosystem first, who will be the Android of the robot era. The path that mobile phones took, robots are now re-tracing.

Five tracks · How we used each company's technologythe five tracks
Track 09Dimensional · DimOS
Connect DimOS as an L3 Provider to the kernel below—using its GO2 multi-robot/formation, SLAM+A* navigation, mechanical arm adaptation, --replay replay. Even its built-in dimos-viewer visualization, we also treat as a regular L1 App, following the same protocol, subject to the same security arbitration.
Track 12AgileX hardware
The entire Demo runs on their hardware: PiPER six-axis arm, NERO second arm, Limo Pro chassis. They are also our first commercialization station—helping B-end manufacturers add another selling point to existing hardware.
Track 20D-Robotics · RDK compute
The entire system runs on RDK S100—Ubuntu 22.04, aarch64, PREEMPT_RT real-time kernel, BPU computing power. The safety emergency stop channel relies on its real-time scheduling. Two teams have already planned to use its BPU to run local vision/language large models.
Track 22viaim voice
Connect viaim earphones to become a voice input App: one sentence becomes a standard intent via AI gateway, commanding any brand's robot on site, without taking out phone, without recognizing brand.First make it as L1 App, after verification, sink the voice capability into kernel, becoming a system-level voice channel.
Track 23Zilo Whisper Ring input
The ring's six-axis IMU + microphone + button, through nring BLE SDK, becomes a first-class control source in the system. Gestures directly drive the PiPER robotic arm; next step: sink "safe gesture" into the security layer—one gesture to reclaim control, making the robot stop.
